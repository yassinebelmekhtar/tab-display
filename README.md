tab-display is a free application that lets you use your Android tablet as a wired, low-latency second display for your Mac. It supports pen and touch input, so you can draw, write, and navigate with ease. No programming knowledge is needed to get started.

## ✨ What is tab-display?

Do you need more screen space but don’t want to buy a second monitor? tab-display turns your Android tablet into an extra display for your Mac. Connect the tablet with a USB cable, and the app mirrors or extends your Mac desktop to the tablet’s screen. Because it uses a wired connection, you get very low latency – meaning the picture and your actions stay in sync. You can use your finger or a stylus to interact with the tablet screen just like a touchscreen monitor.

The app is built with modern technology to keep the video smooth and responsive. It is designed for artists, students, office workers, and anyone who wants a portable second screen.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Wired connection** – Uses a USB cable for a stable, fast link between your Mac and tablet. No Wi-Fi needed.
- **Low latency** – The screen updates almost instantly, so you can draw or scroll without noticeable delay.
- **Pen and touch support** – Use your tablet’s stylus or your finger to click, drag, and draw on the second screen.
- **High-quality video** – Uses efficient video compression (HEVC) to keep the image sharp while using little bandwidth.
- **Easy setup** – No complicated network settings. Just plug in and run.
- **Works with many Android tablets** – Supports tablets running Android 8.0 or later.
- **Made for macOS** – Optimised for Mac computers with Apple silicon or Intel processors.

## 💻 System Requirements

Before you download, make sure your setup meets these simple requirements:

- **Mac:** macOS 11 (Big Sur) or later. Both Apple silicon (M1, M2, etc.) and Intel Macs are supported.
- **Android tablet:** Android 8.0 (Oreo) or later. The tablet must support USB tethering or USB accessory mode (most modern tablets do).
- **Cable:** A USB data cable to connect the tablet to your Mac. (A charging-only cable will not work.)
- **Storage:** About 100 MB of free space on your Mac for the application.
- **Permissions:** You may need to allow the app to control your Mac’s display and accessibility features the first time you run it.

If you are unsure whether your tablet is compatible, check the tablet’s settings for “USB preferences” or “Developer options” – the app will guide you if needed.

## 🖱️ How to Use

Using tab-display is as simple as plugging in a cable. Here’s how to set it up:

1. **Connect your tablet** to your Mac using the USB cable. Make sure the cable is securely plugged into both devices.

2. **Enable USB debugging** on your Android tablet (if you haven’t already). To do this:
   - Open Settings on your tablet.
   - Go to “About tablet” and tap “Build number” seven times. This unlocks “Developer options”.
   - Go back to Settings, open “Developer options”, and turn on “USB debugging”.
   - When you connect the tablet to your Mac, a pop-up on the tablet will ask “Allow USB debugging?” – tap “Allow”.

3. **Open tab-display** on your Mac (if it isn’t already running). The app will detect your tablet automatically.

4. **Choose your display mode** – you can either mirror your Mac screen (the tablet shows exactly what’s on your Mac) or extend it (the tablet becomes a second, separate screen). Select your preference in the app window.

5. **Start using the second screen** – drag windows over to the tablet, draw with your stylus, or tap with your finger. The app will remember your settings for next time.

Tip: For the best experience, keep the tablet plugged into power (if your tablet supports charging while using USB data) and adjust the display resolution in the app to match your tablet’s screen.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

If something doesn’t work, try these common fixes:

- **The tablet is not detected:** Check that the USB cable is a data cable, not a charging-only cable. Try a different USB port on your Mac. Make sure USB debugging is enabled on the tablet and that you tapped “Allow” on the tablet’s pop-up.
- **The screen is laggy or blurry:** Lower the resolution or quality setting in the tab-display app. Close other heavy apps on your Mac. Use a shorter, higher-quality USB cable.
- **Pen or touch doesn’t work:** Ensure the tablet’s screen is unlocked. Some tablets require you to enable “Pointer input” or “Touch input” in the app’s settings.
- **The app won’t open:** Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and check if tab-display is blocked. Click “Open Anyway”. Also make sure you have granted screen recording permission.
- **Audio issues:** tab-display does not transfer audio. You will still hear sound from your Mac’s speakers. This is normal.

If you still have problems, visit the releases page and check the “Issues” tab for help from other users.

**Does it work with Windows?**  
tab-display is made for Mac computers only. It does not run on Windows.

**Will it work with my iPad?**
